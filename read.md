# H·E·N·R·Y™ Android
### Hyperintelligence Engine Neural Reasoning Yield

> A fully native Android AI assistant with voice, vision, brain training, autonomous multi-format document engine, screen recording studio, device control, biometric security, and live real-time trackers — built on free services. No subscription. No API key needed on device.

**Version:** 25.0.0 · **Min Android:** 7.0 (API 24) · **Target:** Android 14 (API 34)

## Key Capabilities (v25.0.0)

- 🎙️ **Voice & Speech Intelligence**: Hands-free wake word ("HENRY"), 6 selectable TTS voices/accents (American, British, Filipino, French, Australian, Japanese), emotion detection, and voice translation across 30+ languages.
- 📄 **Autonomous Document Engine (`HenryFileEngine`)**: Instant on-device generation of **PDF**, **Word (.docx)**, **PowerPoint (.pptx)**, **Excel (.xlsx)**, and **Markdown (.md)** documents with APA 7 research citations, tables, and formatted headers.
- 👁️ **Vision Intelligence & Multi-Attachment**: Live camera ML Kit analysis, object detection & real-time crosshair tracking, facial recognition, OCR text scanning, animal scanner, and simultaneous multi-image inspection (up to 10 photos or PDF documents).
- 🎥 **HD Screen Recording Studio**: Background screen capture service with floating draggable HUD, pause/resume, and studio metadata generator for YouTube/TikTok scripts.
- 🔒 **Biometric Security & Stealth Mode**: Hardware biometric authentication (facial recognition and fingerprint) on startup or app lock, plus stealth mode.
- 🚀 **Space & Astronomy Radar**: Live International Space Station (ISS) orbital tracker, NASA Near-Earth Asteroid close-approach radar, and NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD).
- ✈️ **Real-Time Live Trackers**: Live flight tracking with departure/arrival status & speed, real-time sports scoreboards (NBA, Football, Premier League), and multi-courier package tracking (DHL, FedEx, UPS, Aramex).
- 🧠 **9-Region Cognitive Brain Hub**: Mental Imagery with 8 audio-guided journeys, Neural Plasticity drills (Stroop, Dual N-Back), Default Mode Network (DMN) reflection, and Sensory Substitution (Color-to-Sound & vibrotactile Braille).
- 🌍 **Geospatial & 3D Earth Globe**: Interactive 3D WebGL Earth globe, turn-by-turn navigation, nearby amenities search, and direct ride hailing integration (Uber, Careem, Grab, Lyft).
- 💰 **Finance & Productivity Suite**: Real-time crypto (CoinGecko), spot commodities (gold & crude oil), 170+ currency forex converter, voice expense ledger, AES-256 password vault, and prioritized to-do manager.

## Architecture

- **Language**: 100% Native Kotlin & Java
- **UI System**: Jetpack Compose & Native Custom Views (`OrbView`, `HenryBrainView`, `HudTickerView`)
- **Document Engine**: Custom zero-dependency pure Java ZIP/XML packaging for DOCX, PPTX, XLSX and native PDF rendering
- **Vision Engine**: Google ML Kit (Vision Labeling, Object Detection, Face Mesh, Barcode Scanning)
- **Database**: Local SQLite and AES-256 encrypted SharedPreferences
- **Networking**: OkHttp3 with asynchronous streaming and fallbacks
